The notes state that most RGS proteins act as GAPs for Gi/o and Gq rather than Gαs (an exception such as RGS-PX1 may be mentioned), and name the mechanisms that do switch Gs signalling off: the intrinsic GTPase of Gαs, GRK/β-arrestin desensitisation and phosphodiesterases.
